## About the role

We are looking for a senior designer who can take a brand from a blank page to a system that holds up everywhere it appears. You will work directly with our **Creative Director** on every project.

This is a freelance role, booked project by project. See our [work](https://studios.houseofsingh.com/work) for the standard we hold.

## Responsibilities

- Develop identity concepts from the strategy brief
- Build logo, type and colour systems that scale
- Write and design brand guidelines a team can follow without us

## Requirements

- Five or more years designing brand identities
- A portfolio of complete identity systems, not single logos
- Clear written communication

## Nice to have

- Experience with packaging or signage
