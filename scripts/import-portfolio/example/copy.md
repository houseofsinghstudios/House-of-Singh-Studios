## Overview
Harbour and Pine is a guesthouse and kitchen on the coast. It had grown room by room, and its look had grown the same way.

We gave it one identity that holds from the front door to the jam jar.

## The Challenge
Every part of the business had its own logo, its own colours and its own voice. Guests met a different brand at every turn.

The owners wanted **one clear identity** without losing the warmth that made people come back.

## Approach
A brand should feel like the place before you arrive.

We started on site, with the rooms, the kitchen and the people who run them. Every decision was tested against how the place actually works.

Read more about our process on the [services page](https://studios.houseofsingh.com/services).

## Process
We built a quiet master mark and a small set of materials: oak, sea green and a warm off white. Each part of the business uses the same pieces in its own way.

## Results
The guesthouse, the kitchen and the shop now read as **one place**. Guests notice the change before they read a word.
